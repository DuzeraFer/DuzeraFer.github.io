Hello,

I'm a technical artist at Kreativitas Studio, working on mobile, web and VR games in
Unity, plain HTML and Unreal. Three of them are live on Poki — Snake vs Human, Harvest
Loop and Butterfly Catch — and nine more are on Google Play, most of which never got a
full launch.

I've also worked on VR and Steam titles as a freelancer, doing 3D models, optimisation,
VFX and materials.

My major accomplishments:

- Twelve shipped games, three of them on Poki. Snake vs Human passed 10 million players
  and was the most-played game of the Green Game Jam; I was on it from the first
  prototype and helped shape its concept and design.
- Optimisation work that gained around 200% more FPS on low-end Android, and the port
  that took the original game from Android to web.
- Conceptualised and built a large body of VFX, shaders and technical animation across
  every game I've worked on.
- Tools that other artists and level designers use daily, including our own tween library
  built to fit the studio's needs, a custom atlas creator, and a UI system reusable on
  every project.
- Mentoring artists into the industry at Casulo Indie · UpLink.
- Tools published on the Unity Asset Store, including Simple VAT, which I wrote to cut
  the number of skinned meshes in web games.

I came into technical art from 3D — Maya, ZBrush, Substance, Photoshop — so I can author
an asset and then fix the pipeline that exports it. Knowing the pipeline is also what
makes it easy to see where it can be improved, which is why I keep building tools, for
myself and for others, to take the repetition out of the work. AI is a big part of that
pipeline now: mostly ChatGPT for concepts and sketching ideas, and Claude Code for coding
and for building the tools that save me time.

I'm based in Brazil and looking for remote work. My portfolio is at
eduardo-fernandes-3d.github.io, and I'd be glad to walk you through any of it.

Thanks for reading,
